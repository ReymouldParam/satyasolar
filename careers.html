Considering a career in solar energy?

We offer multiple oppurtunities to build a rewarding career with Krisol Solar, where innovation, sustainability, and growth come together.

Krisol Solar Infra Pvt Ltd is a professionally managed renewable energy company delivering comprehensive solar power solutions across Telangana and Andhra Pradesh. The company specializes in the design, engineering, procurement, installation, and maintenance of solar rooftop and power generation systems for residential, commercial, and industrial clients. With a strong commitment to quality, operational excellence, and sustainable development, Krisol Solar Infra Pvt Ltd plays an active role in supporting India’s clean energy initiatives.
